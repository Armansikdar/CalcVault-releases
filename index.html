How it works

Four steps. Two minutes.

Install

Download the APK and allow your phone to install it.

Set your PIN

Choose your own secret PIN on first launch. Nothing is set for you.

Add photos

Pick photos and videos. They are encrypted and moved into the vault.

Use it like a calculator

Type your PIN on the keypad and the vault opens. Anyone else just sees a calculator.

Honest about what it can and can't do

Good privacy tools tell you their limits.

  • Remember your PIN and your backup password. There is no cloud copy and no reset, so we can't recover your files for you.
  • Make a backup. If you lose or reset your phone, the vault goes with it. CalcVault reminds you to back up.
  • A phone that is rooted or infected with spyware can't be fully protected by any app. Keep your phone's own lock screen on.
  • CalcVault is a young project and it is improving quickly. Tell us what breaks.

Questions

Short answers.

Is it free?

Yes. There are no accounts, no subscriptions and no ads inside the vault.

Do my photos go to a server?

No. Your files are stored encrypted on your own phone. We don't run a server for them. The app uses the internet only for the browser, update checks and, if you choose to send one, a support message.

What if I forget my PIN?

There's no reset, by design: anyone who could reset it could also open your vault. If you set up a fingerprint or made a backup, you still have a way in.

Which phones does it work on?

Android 7.0 and newer.

Why do I have to install it from outside Google Play?

CalcVault is currently shared as a direct download. Your phone will ask once to allow installing apps from your browser. Only download it from this page.

Privacy policy

Plain language, last updated November 2026.

What stays on your phone

Your photos, videos, PINs and encryption keys are stored on your device only. We cannot see them, and we do not upload them anywhere.

What the app sends over the internet

  • Update check: the app reads a small public file from GitHub to see if a new version exists.
  • Support messages: only if you press Send in the Support box. The text you wrote (and your phone model and Android version, if you tick that box) is sent to us. We don't collect your name, email or contacts. Please don't put your PIN or private details in it.
  • Browser: pages you open in the built-in browser load from the websites you visit.

Permissions

Camera (intruder photo), photos and videos (to add them to the vault), notifications and storage (downloads and backups), biometrics (fingerprint unlock) and the internet (above). You can remove any permission in your phone's settings.
